# Adjoa Yeboah Clothing — Website Concept

Independent, uncommissioned website concept. It is **not an official brand website** and must not be presented as approved or operated by the business until written authorization is obtained.

## Pages
- `index.html` — editorial homepage and featured styles
- `shop.html` — searchable and filterable catalogue
- `product.html` — dynamic product detail page
- `cart.html` — browser-local demo cart (no checkout or payment)
- `about.html` — draft brand story based on a third-party published profile
- `contact.html` — public contact details flagged for owner verification
- `styles.css` and `app.js` — responsive design and interactions

## Public research used
- Current public storefront: https://ayc.fashion/ — public product names and displayed prices are provisional examples only and must be reconfirmed by the brand.
- Google Maps listing: 1 Levites Avenue, Accra, Ghana; +233 24 122 4404. Other directories show different information, so verify before publishing.

- Published designer profile: https://maakola.com/artisans/adjoa-yeboah/ — profile describes the founder's journey, 2013 company founding and 2015 Vlisco collaboration; brand approval is required for final official copy.

## Launch limitations
- Editorial images are illustrative stock photography, not verified product photos.
- Cart is a local demo only. No checkout, payment gateway, stock sync, customer accounts, or order tracking is connected.
- Contact form opens an email draft; it does not send or store submissions.
- No reviews, policies, lead times, shipping promises, product specifications, or payment arrangements have been invented.
- Before official launch, obtain written owner approval, approved logo and images, verified product catalogue, prices, sizing, availability, contact details, store policies, domain access, payment setup, and privacy/legal review.
